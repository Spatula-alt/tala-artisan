How to design a trophy
that actually works.

Most awards go wrong in the sticker, not the body. This is the order we walk customers through, and the mistakes we see most often.

STEP 01

Start from the occasion, not the shape

Decide what the award is for before you pick anything. A pageant centrepiece and forty participation awards are different objects with different budgets.

  • A pageant → the Lady or Gentleman figure, large, on a gold bar base.
  • A whole division → plaque or arch, small, one colour, identical. The set matters more than the piece.
  • A thank-you → a small acrylic blade on a sprayed box base still looks generous.
STEP 02

Build the tiers together

If there are places, design all of them at once or they will not look like a family.

  • Keep one shape and one base, and change only the height: 28 cm, 22 cm, 17 cm.
  • Or keep one height and step the sheet: gold mirror, silver mirror, then a solid colour.
  • Do not change both at once. It stops reading as a ranking and starts looking random.
STEP 03

Use the colour — it is free

We print, not engrave, so full colour costs the same as black — and you can put a photo in it. This is the one real advantage over an engraved award, and most people forget it.

  • Put the school, barangay or team colours in the sticker background.
  • Sponsor logos print in their proper colours, which sponsors notice.
  • Drop a photo into the designer to see it in place. Badge puts a portrait at the top; Background fills the sticker.
  • Check contrast: light text on a light sheet disappears under hall lighting.
STEP 04

Get the four lines in the right order

Trophy wording reads top to bottom in a fixed order. Changing it confuses people.

  • Line 1 — who is giving it: the league, school, LGU or company.
  • Line 2 — the award itself, the largest line on the sticker.
  • Line 3 — who or what it is for. Full name, or the category.
  • Line 4 — when or where. Write the month out: 12 April 2026, not 04/12/26.
STEP 05

Pick the font for the room

The font does more work than the shape. Match it to the event, not to your taste.

  • Sport — condensed and loud. Liga, palaro, intramurals.
  • Formal — service awards, academic honours, civic recognition.
  • Script — pageants and coronation nights. Keep the name short; script needs room.
  • On the figure shapes the sticker sits on the base, which is wide and short. Three lines read better there than four.
  • Matte laminate if there will be a photographer. Gloss throws flash straight back.
STEP 06

Read the proof like a stranger

This is the last moment anything is free. We reprint our own errors at no charge, but an approved spelling becomes a new order.

  • Check names against a registration or payroll list, not from memory.
  • Read the date out loud — wrong years are the commonest error we catch.
  • Watch middle initials, suffixes (Jr., III) and Ñ / ñ.
  • Have someone who was not involved read it once.

Before you order

Is it engraved or printed?

Printed. We cut the shape from coloured acrylic sheet, print your artwork in full colour on vinyl, laminate it, cut it to shape and apply it by hand. That is why colour, logos, team shades and photographs cost nothing extra — engraving cannot do any of those.

Why are there no cup or star shapes?

Because we cut flat sheet rather than mould it. A cup would have to be moulded, and shapes with thin arms or points crack at the narrow part — first in our hands, then in yours. Everything we offer is cut as one piece with no weak section.

A PVC junction box as a base?

Yes, and it is one of our better ideas. An electrical junction box is moulded to a tight tolerance, costs very little, weighs nothing in a courier box and takes spray paint beautifully. Sprayed black or silver, nobody has ever guessed what it started as.

What happens to the photo I dropped in?

Nothing leaves your device. The designer reads the file in your own browser to draw the preview — it is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. When you order, send us the original file and we print from that.

Will the sticker peel off?

Not in normal indoor use. The lamination is what protects it; an unlaminated sticker is what curls at the edges after a year. Keep it out of direct sun and off a car dashboard, and do not scrub it with solvent.

Can I really order just one?

Yes. There is no minimum. Volume only changes the unit price, not whether we take the job. A single piece still gets a free proof.

Is the price in the designer final?

It is an estimate covering the body, printing, your quantity, turnaround and shipping. VAT and any special packing are confirmed on the written quote we send with the proof. The courier can also run a day either side of the date shown.

Can every piece have a different name?

Yes, at no extra cost, as long as the title and layout stay the same. Send the names as a list or a spreadsheet and we will match them to the proof line by line.

What if a name is printed wrong?

If it differs from the proof you approved, we reprint and reapply free, usually the same week. If the approved proof carried the mistake, it is charged as a new sticker — which is far cheaper than a new trophy, since the acrylic and base are reused.

Can you match trophies we bought before?

Usually. Send photographs with a ruler or a known object in frame, plus the overall height. We will tell you honestly whether it is a close match or only a near one.
