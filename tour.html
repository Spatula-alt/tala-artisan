A quick tour

From idea to trophy, on one screen.

Tala Artisan makes custom acrylic trophies by hand in Gloria, Oriental Mindoro and ships them nationwide. Here is how ordering one works — from designing it in 3D to holding it.

  • From ₱500 a piece
  • Free proof before we cut
  • No minimum order
01 · The 3D designer

Design it yourself, in 3D.

Start from an occasion — pageant, liga, school, fiesta, corporate — then change anything. The trophy is a real 3D model you can turn and zoom.

  • 17 cut shapes, including pageant and sports figures
  • 11 acrylic colours, gold and silver mirror, 3 sizes
  • 4 bases in 6 finishes
02 · The printed sticker

Your words, printed live.

Type the event, title, name and date. The full-colour sticker redraws on the 3D trophy as you type, so you see whether a long name fits before anything is printed.

  • Sticker shapes, colour schemes and 5 fonts
  • Add a motif — crown, laurel, star — or your own logo
  • Drop in a photo and it prints straight onto the sticker
03 · Your own shape

Cut it from your own picture.

Upload a mascot, a logo or a photo of a person. The background is removed automatically and the rest becomes the cut shape — with the picture printed on it if you like.

  • Automatic background removal, or touch it up by hand
  • Thin parts are thickened so the acrylic will not snap
04 · No guessing

The price and the date, instantly.

Every change updates the total and the day it arrives — quantity, speed, delivery area and payment included. Bulk orders cost less per piece.

  • Rush in 3 days, standard in 7, economy in 14
  • Pickup in Gloria, or shipped anywhere in the Philippines
  • Cash, GCash, PayMaya or bank transfer
05 · On any phone

Order from your phone.

The whole site works on mobile. Browse the shop, design your trophy and send it to the studio in a few minutes — no app to install, no account needed.

06 · How ordering works

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. 1

    Send your design

    Tap "Send this design to the studio", or just message us the names, quantity and date.

  2. 2

    Approve the free proof

    A digital mock-up within 2 days. Nothing is cut until you say yes.

  3. 3

    We make it by hand

    Cut, printed, laminated and assembled in 7 working days. Rush in 3.

  4. 4

    Pickup or shipping

    Free pickup in Gloria, or shipped nationwide — next day around Mindoro.
