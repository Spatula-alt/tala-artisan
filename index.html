Four steps, no surprises.

Nothing is cut until you have seen a proof and said it is right. If we misspell something on our side, we re-cut it free.

  1. Send the wording

    Use the designer, or just message us the names, the quantity and the date you need them. Send a logo if you have one — colour costs nothing extra.

  2. Approve the proof

    A digital mock-up comes back within 2 working days, free. Spellings we are unsure of come back as questions, not as printed mistakes.

  3. We cut, print and apply

    Sheet cut to shape, artwork printed in full colour, laminated and applied by hand. Seven working days as standard, three for rush work, subject to the schedule.

  4. Collect or we deliver

    Boxed, and labelled per recipient if you need them sorted. Free studio pickup, next day across Oriental Mindoro, three days to the rest of Luzon.

Tala means star.

We put one on the back of everything that leaves the workshop — originally just our own mark that a piece had been checked. Customers started asking for it on the front instead, so now it is on the menu.

We work out of Calapan City and most of what we make stays on the island: barangay and municipal liga, school recognition days across the division, fiesta competitions, pageants, co-op awards for growers around Victoria and Naujan, and a steady run of service plaques for people finishing thirty years in local government.

Studio visits by appointment. We are a short ride from Calapan Port, which takes the roll-on/roll-off ferries from Batangas.

What we work in

Acrylic sheet
5 to 8 mm cast sheet in blue, red, green, yellow, orange, white, black and tan. All one price.
Clear & mirrored
Clear, gold mirror and silver mirror cost a little more. Mirror scratches, so handle it by the edges.
Printed vinyl
Full colour on white vinyl, laminated gloss or matte, cut to shape and applied by hand.
Gold bar base
Solid wood, cut and finished as a bullion bar. Our most ordered base.
PVC box bases
Octagon junction boxes and rectangular utility boxes, sprayed. Light, cheap and smarter than they sound.
Plain block
A stepped wooden block. The cheapest and the most traditional.

Good to know

Artwork
SVG, PDF or AI is best. PNG or JPG works at 300 dpi or better. Colour costs nothing extra.
Smallest text
2 mm cap height. Below that the lamination softens the edges.
Photos
We can print a photo straight into the sticker. Send the original file, 1000 px or wider.
Turnaround

Payment

Reprints
