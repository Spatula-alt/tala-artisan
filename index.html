How it works.

Nothing is cut until you approve the proof. If a mistake is ours, we remake it free.

  1. 01 · Today

    Send the wording

    Use the designer, or message us the names, quantity and date. Add a logo if you have one.

  2. 02 · Within 2 days

    Approve the proof

    A free digital mock-up. Any spelling we are unsure of comes back as a question.

  3. 03 · 7 working days

    We make it

    Cut, printed, laminated and assembled by hand. Rush in 3 days.

  4. 04 · Pickup or delivery

    Collect or we deliver

    Free studio pickup. Next day across Mindoro, 3 days to Luzon.
